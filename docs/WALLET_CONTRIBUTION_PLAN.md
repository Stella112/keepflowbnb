# Actual-wallet contribution planning

`POST /api/wallet/plan` accepts only `{bellId,bellVersion,bellContentHash,contributionUsd}`. A current authenticated wallet session supplies the wallet; no wallet, account or policy is accepted from the client. The selected immutable Bell version must match its stored content hash. The endpoint checks origin, bounds the request stream to 1024 bytes, rate limits actual reads and returns private no-store responses.

The response `{plan}` has `mode: READ_ONLY`, `executionEnabled: false` and `financialAuthorization: NONE`. Provenance includes chain 56, consistent balance block, read time, indicative market time and tracked-asset scope. Personal and stricter effective Bell policy snapshots accompany the result. The hypothetical new contribution is added only in the calculation, never to real balances or paper accounts.

Integer micro-dollar calculations reserve the ceiling of the effective cash requirement, purchase only target underweights beyond drift threshold, and allocate largest deficits first with alphabetical ties. Each company receives at most one proposed leg bounded by its target deficit, effective trade cap, single-company exposure room and remaining hypothetical contribution. Existing cash is never used beyond the hypothetical contribution. Existing stock holdings are never sold; omitted Bell assets remain visible with zero target. Rounding and blocked allocations stay in cash. Snapshot consistency and freshness are required; a 60-second planning freshness ceiling applies when no price cap is selected.

Amounts and projected weights are indicative planning facts. Provider availability, executable quotes, fees, slippage, simulation, wallet authorization and settlement remain separate checks. Configured daily/weekly ceilings bound cumulative proposed purchases, but live period usage/reservations are not integrated, so `budgetAvailability: UNVERIFIED` is always explicit. `projectedReserveShortfallUsd` identifies unresolved reserve deficits without implying execution readiness. This is not an approved or executable live Autopilot plan, schedule or quote. No signer, broadcaster, paid provider or real-money operation is called.

Verification: `node --test tests/wallet-contribution.test.ts` covers underweight allocation, no automatic selling, immutable inputs, stricter policy intersection, exact micro-dollar remainders, reserve rounding, no use of existing cash without contribution, and invalid/stale/future snapshot rejection. Fixtures are synthetic unit tests, not evidence of real balances.
